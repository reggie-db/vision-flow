# Vision Flow branding concepts

These three packages position Vision Flow as a Databricks-native product without copying the Databricks symbol. They share a restrained enterprise palette, dense product language, and diagram styles that fit beside AppKit, AI/BI dashboards, and Databricks architecture material.

Vision Flow turns images into governed, structured records. A user describes the data to extract, the system creates or reuses a versioned schema, a vision model processes the image, and the typed result becomes available for review and analysis.

## Shared product definition

**Category:** schema-defined visual data ingestion

**Short description:** Vision Flow uses reusable schemas and dynamically generated prompts to convert images into typed, reviewable data on Databricks.

**Core workflow:**

1. Describe the image category and required fields.
2. Create or match a versioned extraction schema.
3. Validate and submit an image to a vision model.
4. Store the typed result with its schema fingerprint and confidence data.
5. Review and analyze results through Databricks SQL, AI/BI dashboards, or Genie.

**Reference use cases:**

- Lottery receipt scanning: extract jurisdiction, game, draw date, ticket quantities, price, retailer, and transaction details.
- Store layout auditing: compare shelf, display, signage, and product placement images with required layouts and record compliance findings.
- ID verification: extract identity fields, check required document characteristics, and route uncertain results for review.
- Operational forms: structure inspection records, signed forms, labels, invoices, and other image-based inputs.

## Shared Databricks alignment

- Use coral red as the main product accent, supported by dark navy and cool neutral surfaces.
- Use red for selection, primary actions, and brand recognition. Keep success, warning, and error colors semantic.
- Favor flat surfaces, thin borders, small radii, and direct labels over decorative gradients or large promotional panels.
- Use diagrams built from images, schemas, arrows, and tables. Avoid neural-network imagery, glowing AI effects, and generic sparkle symbols.
- Do not reproduce or modify the Databricks symbol. Vision Flow needs a distinct mark that can appear beside it.

## Review criteria

Choose the package that best answers these questions:

1. Should the brand lead with the reusable data pipeline, the capture experience, or governance?
2. Should the product name remain two words or become the compact `VisionFlow` wordmark?
3. Will the primary audience see the brand first in an architecture presentation, the scanner application, or a compliance workflow?
4. Does the mark remain recognizable at favicon size and in a single color?

The package SVGs are concept boards, not production logo exports. Final artwork should include optical spacing, small-size variants, and accessible light and dark treatments.
